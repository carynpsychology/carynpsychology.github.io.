How we can support you

Specialised care tailored to your unique psychological needs, blending evidence-based techniques with human connection.

When holding it together feels exhausting

  • Anxiety, overthinking, burnout, or feeling disconnected from what matters
  • A space to pause, breathe, and make sense of what's weighing on you

Understanding yourself and your relationships

  • Relationship stress, communication struggles, or repeating patterns
  • More clarity, and more satisfying connections

Navigating neurodiversity

  • Understanding your needs and finding strategies
  • Possibly, formal assessment if it would help you move forward
Things can feel very different on the inside,
even when everything looks okay.

We can help you make sense of what's been carried, with thoughtful, professional support.

What to expect

We don't focus on fixing you. We focus on understanding — and helping you move forward in a way that is practical and meaningful.

Initial Consultation

A space to share what's been going on and begin making sense of it.

  • 60–90 minute session
  • Space to share what has been going on
  • Explore your experiences and background
  • Begin to notice patterns or difficulties
  • Consider next steps together

Therapy (Most common)

Working through thoughts, emotions, and experiences with support.

  • Understand thoughts, emotions, and patterns
  • Support for anxiety, low mood, burnout, OCD, phobias
  • Develop ways to cope and respond differently
  • A space to talk openly and reflect

Ongoing Therapy

Steady, ongoing work that adapts as your needs evolve.

  • Sessions at a pace that suits you
  • Work that adapts as your needs change
  • Deepen understanding over time
  • Build steady, lasting change

Assessment (When needed)

A structured process to better understand specific concerns.

  • For ADHD, autism, learning differences
  • Often for children and adolescents
  • Uses structured tools and observations
  • Usually 2–5 sessions, depending on individual needs
  • In-depth understanding of specific concerns

Understanding the Findings Together

A guided session to help you understand and move forward with clarity.

  • Guided walkthrough of the findings
  • Clear understanding of strengths and challenges
  • Diagnosis, where appropriate
  • Tailored recommendations for home, school, and daily life
  • Guidance for caregivers and key supports
  • 3–5 page summary for school or formal use

Meet your Therapist

Hi, I'm Caryn. I work with individuals who feel like something isn't quite right, even if they can't fully explain why. On the outside, things may look manageable, but internally it can feel overwhelming, stuck, or disconnected.

When something feels "off", I believe it's worth taking the time to understand it. Often, what we struggle with are patterns we've had to learn over time — ways of coping that once made sense, but may no longer fit who we are now.

In our sessions, I offer a space that is genuine, non-judgmental, and gently direct, where you can explore what's going on at your own pace, without needing to filter or have everything figured out.
