When to consider professional support?

You may consider reaching out if:

  • Your results are consistently high
  • Your symptoms are affecting daily functioning
  • You've been feeling this way for some time
  • You would like clarity or guidance

If you'd like support in making sense of your results and what they mean for you, you're welcome to book a session.

Understanding your experiences

If you're not ready to seek support yet, you can start by learning more about what you might be experiencing.

🌿

Depression & Low Mood

Sometimes it's not obvious. It can feel like:

  • Low energy or constant tiredness
  • Losing interest in things you used to enjoy
  • Feeling numb, flat, or disconnected
  • Being hard on yourself

Not always sadness — sometimes just a quiet heaviness that doesn't go away.

🌙

Anxiety & Burnout

This often shows up in people who are still functioning — but exhausted.

You might notice:

  • Overthinking or difficulty "switching off"
  • Feeling on edge, restless, or easily overwhelmed
  • Physical tension, poor sleep, or fatigue
  • Feeling drained even after resting

Burnout can look like:

  • Emotional exhaustion
  • Loss of motivation
  • Feeling detached from work or responsibilities
🧠

Neurodiversity in Adults (Autism/ADHD)

Some people have always felt a little different — without knowing why.

You might relate to:

  • Feeling out of sync with others
  • Masking or over-adapting socially
  • Sensory or emotional overwhelm
  • Struggles with focus, organisation, or routines

Understanding this can bring clarity — and a more compassionate way of seeing yourself.

If any of this resonates with you, it doesn't mean something is "wrong".

It may simply mean there are patterns worth understanding — and support can help.
