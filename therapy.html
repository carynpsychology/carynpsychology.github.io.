Therapy

A space to understand what you're going through — and begin to feel differently.

How Therapy works

Therapy is a space to slow down, talk things through, and begin making sense of what you're experiencing.

You don't need a diagnosis to start.

Together we:
  • Explore what's been feeling difficult or overwhelming
  • Understand patterns, emotions, and underlying causes
  • Work through concerns like anxiety, burnout, OCD, phobias, or low mood

For many adults, clarity and even diagnosis can emerge naturally through this process – without the need for formal testing.

What it feels like:

A conversation that gradually brings insight, relief, and direction.

Our approach

We use an integrative, evidence-based approach, tailored to you.

This may include:

  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)
  • Trauma-Focused CBT
  • Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)
  • Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT)
  • Behavioural strategies and emotional regulation work
  • Mindfulness-based approaches

Rather than applying a single method, we focus on what fits you best — your goals, your pace, and your experiences. Along the way, you can expect:

  • A safe, non-judgmental space
  • Clear understanding of your patterns
  • Practical tools for daily life
  • Support that is both gentle and effective

What happens in Therapy

Initial Consultation (60 – 90 mins)

  • We begin with understanding you — your experiences, patterns, and what's been going on.
  • There's no pressure to "perform" or explain everything perfectly. We take it step by step.

Ongoing Sessions (45 – 60 mins)

We work together to:

  • Understand patterns and underlying causes
  • Make sense of emotions and reactions
  • Build practical tools and coping strategies
  • Create meaningful, sustainable change
