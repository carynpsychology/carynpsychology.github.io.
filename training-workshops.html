Training & Workshops

Whether you're a parent, educator, or organisation, sessions can be tailored to your setting and needs.

Practical, evidence-based support for real world settings

We offer training and workshops designed to help parents, educators, and organisations better understand and support neurodiverse individuals. Our sessions focus on practical strategies you can apply immediately — not just theory.

All trainings can be tailored to your setting, whether you're supporting a classroom, a team, or a community.

Send an Enquiry →

Who we support

Educators

  • Managing behaviour, attention, and emotional regulation in the classroom
  • Supporting ADHD, autism, and diverse learning needs with practical strategies

Parents of neurodiverse children

  • Understanding your child's behaviour, emotions, and needs
  • Learning how to respond in ways that are effective and supportive

Schools, institutions, and organisations

  • Building inclusive environments for neurodiverse individuals
  • Equipping teams with practical, evidence-based approaches

Training areas

Understanding & Identification

  • Recognise early signs and guide families with confidence
  • Identify early indicators of ADHD, autism, and learning differences
  • Distinguish behavioural concerns from underlying developmental needs
  • Communicate concerns to parents clearly and sensitively
  • Provide practical psychoeducation parents can understand and act on
  • Navigate referral pathways based on each child and family context

Support & Intervention

Practical strategies for real-life situations

  • Manage challenging behaviour, meltdowns, and emotional regulation
  • Apply FBA-informed strategies to understand and respond effectively
  • Support attention, transitions, and classroom readiness skills
  • Respond to anxiety, mood, and internal struggles in older students
  • Use clear, consistent approaches across classroom and staff

What to expect

Our trainings are grounded in clinical experience and real-world application — shaped by years of working directly with individuals, families, and neurodiverse populations.

We take an integrative, evidence-informed approach, drawing from:

  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)
  • Behavioural approaches (including ABA-informed strategies)
  • Neurodiversity-affirming practices
  • Developmental and systems perspectives

This allows us to move beyond theory and focus on what actually works in everyday settings. Each training section is:

  • Interactive, discussion-based sessions
  • Practical strategies you can apply immediately
  • Real-life examples and case discussions
  • Tailored to your setting and needs
  • A supportive, non-judgmental space

Available formats include:

  • Talks and seminars
  • Small group workshops
  • Ongoing training series [ Coming Soon ]

Customised for your setting

Every environment is different. We work with you to design training that fits your:

  • Student or client profile
  • Team experience level
  • Specific challenges and goals
